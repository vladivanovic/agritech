### 1.3 Additional Land Costs
- **Registration/Transfer Tax**: 4-6% of purchase price (paid to Legal Affairs Bureau)
- **Fixed Asset Tax (Kōteki Shisanzei)**: 1.4% of assessed value annually
- **City Planning Tax**: 0.3% of assessed value annually
- **Survey Costs**: ¥200,000-500,000 for boundary confirmation

## 5. Funding & Subsidy Options

### 5.1 Government Subsidies (2026)
| Subsidy | Amount | Eligibility | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------------|-------|
| New Farmer Establishment Subsidy | ¥3,000,000 - ¥10,000,000 | First-time farmers under 45 years | Covers equipment, facilities, training |
| Agricultural Land Development Subsidy | ¥500,000 - ¥5,000,000 | Land improvement projects | Requires committee approval |
| Greenhouse Installation Subsidy | ¥1,000,000 - ¥5,000,000 | Those installing greenhouses | 1/2 to 2/3 of cost covered |
| Crop Insurance Subsidy | ¥10,000 - ¥30,000 per ha | All insured farmers | Prefectural subsidy of 1/2 cost |
| Sales Promotion Subsidy | ¥100,000 - ¥500,000 | JA market participants | For branding, packaging, events |

### 5.2 Subsidy Application Tips
- **Apply early**: Some subsidies have yearly quotas, first-come-first-served
- **Keep records**: All receipts, photos of work-in-progress required
- **Consult JA**: JA Fukuoka often provides subsidy application support
- **Language support**: Administrative lawyers can help with application documents

### 5.3 Financing Options
| Option | Interest Rate | Terms | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|-------|
| Japan Finance Corporation (JFC) | 1.5-3.0% | Up to 20 years | Government-owned, favorable to farmers |
| Local bank loans | 2.0-4.0% | 10-15 years | Requires business plan, collateral |
| JA farm loans | 1.5-2.5% | Up to 15 years | For JA members only |
| Private investment | Varies | Flexible | Higher interest, may require KK structure |
