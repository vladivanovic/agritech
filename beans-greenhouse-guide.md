### 2.3 Irrigation & Fertilization
- **Drip irrigation**: 1 emitter per plant (bush) or per hill (pole)
- **Fertilization**: Light feeder; avoid excess nitrogen (reduces flowering)
  - Base: Organic compost 3-5kg/m²
  - Side-dressing: Phosphorus-potassium after flowering
- **EC management**: 1.2-1.8 dS/m
- **pH management**: 6.0-6.8 optimal
- **Watering**: Consistent moisture during flowering/fruiting; reduce slightly at maturity

### 2.4 Pest & Disease Management
- **Major diseases**: Powdery mildew, bacterial blight, root rot (Phytophthora)
- **Major pests**: Aphids, bean beetles, spider mites
- **Prevention**:
  - 50-60% humidity management
  - Good air circulation (avoid overcrowding)
  - Crop rotation (2+ years before replanting beans)
  - Disease-free seed
  - Row covers early season (remove at flowering for pollination)
- **Organic options**: Neem oil, potassium bicarbonate, beneficial insects (ladybugs, parasitic wasps)

### 2.5 Harvest & Post-Harvest
- **Pole beans**: Harvest 60-70 days after planting; pick young pods (10-15cm)
- **Bush beans**: Harvest 50-60 days after planting; pick regularly to encourage production
- **Yield**: 2-3kg per m² for pole beans; 1.5-2kg per m² for bush beans
- **Storage**: 1 week refrigerated; blanch and freeze for longer storage
- **Note**: Beans fix nitrogen; excellent crop to precede heavy feeders like tomatoes
