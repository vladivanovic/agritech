## Content Summary

### Key Findings in Research

**Legal Requirements:**
- Foreigners require permanent residency (永住者) or Special Agricultural Practitioner status
- Land ownership blocked under temporary/work visas
- MAFF notification and Prefecture Agricultural Committee approval mandatory

**Budget Ranges:**
| Farm Type | Initial Capital | Monthly Operating |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Citrus Orchard | $800k–$1.1M | ~$345k/month |
| Vegetable Farm | $180k–$220k | ~$300k/month |

**Support Programs:**
- Agricultural Start-Up Subsidies: up to ¥3M for equipment
- Land acquisition discounts available
- Foreign Farmer Training grants: up to ¥2M
- 6-week Farming Start-up Course with ¥1M support

**Critical Path Items:**
1. Verify permanent residency status
2. Engage Japanese-speaking agronomist
3. Secure budget approval ($100k–$200k seed money)
4. Initiate property tours with extension office
