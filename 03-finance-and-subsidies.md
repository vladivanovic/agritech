## 1. The real support stack

These are the genuine national programs for new farmers (MAFF, 新規就農者育成総合対策）. Program names changed in 2022 — anything calling them 準備型・経営開始型 is using the old 農業次世代人材投資資金-era names.

| Program | What it actually is | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **就農準備資金** | Grant *during training* at a recognised institution: **¥137,500/month (max ¥1.65M/year), up to 2 years** | Age ≤49 in principle; training at a recognised institution (the Munakata 機構's 新規就農研修 is the local route) |
| **経営開始資金** | Grant *after starting*: **¥137,500/month (max ¥1.65M/year), up to 3 years → ¥4.95M maximum total**. It is **income support paid monthly**, not a lump sum and not an equipment subsidy | 認定新規就農者 status (municipality-certified 青年等就農計画）; start age ≤49; prior-year household income ≲ ¥6M; land positioned in the municipality's 目標地図 / via the 農地バンク， etc. |
| **経営発展支援事業** | **Capital** subsidy for machinery, facilities and **fruit-tree planting**: eligible project cost capped at **¥10M** (**¥5M** if also receiving 経営開始資金）; support up to **3/4** of cost (national share ≤1/2 plus prefectural top-up), i.e. max grant ¥7.5M / ¥3.75M | 認定新規就農者； approved project plan |
| **青年等就農資金** (Japan Finance Corporation) | **Interest-free (0%) loan**, up to **¥37M** (¥100M in special cases), up to 17 years | 認定新規就農者. **Expressly excludes farmland purchase** |
| Tax relief on acquisition | Registration/acquisition-tax reductions exist for farmland acquired under a 農用地利用集積等促進計画 (via the 農地バンク route) | Route-dependent — ask the committee secretariat |
| 農業共済 / 収入保険 (NOSAI) | Crop and revenue insurance | National treasury bears ~50% of premiums by statute |

Deleted as fabricated (FACTCHECK.md #18–28): "Agricultural Start-Up Subsidies up to ¥3M", "Foreign Farmer Training grants up to ¥2M" (no nationality-earmarked program exists anywhere in the stack), "6-week Farming Start-up Course with ¥1M support", "land acquisition discounts", the five named equipment subsidies in the old equipment doc, and a "New Farmer Establishment Subsidy ¥3–10M for under-45s" (the age test is ≤49 at start, and 45 is a different definition inside the certification rules). Also deleted: interest-bearing "JFC new farmer loans at 1.5–3.0%" as the headline product — for certified new farmers the flagship JFC loan is the 0% 青年等就農資金 above.

## 2. Honest eligibility assessment for THIS project

Read §1 carefully and a problem is immediately visible: **almost the entire support stack is gated on 認定新規就農者 certification**, which certifies a 青年等就農計画 — a plan to make farming your **principal occupation**. This project is explicitly a **side business alongside a full-time salaried job**, and:

- the "farming as main occupation" premise of certification does not fit;
- 経営開始資金 additionally tests **household income (≲¥6M in the prior year)** — a household with a full-time professional salary will typically exceed it;
- the 0% JFC loan excludes land purchase anyway, which is this project's biggest single cost.

**Therefore the financial model in §4 is built with zero subsidy income.** That is the conservative, honest basis for a side business. If circumstances change (e.g. a future move to farming as the main occupation), the stack in §1 becomes highly relevant — it is more generous than the fabricated version it replaces — and certification is applied for at **宗像市農林水産課**, separately from the Article 3 filing. Whether any municipal or prefectural top-up exists for a part-time/side-business entrant is an **open question to ask at pre-consultation**; do not budget for an answer that has not been given.

## 4. The rebuilt financial model (purchase, 0.4 ha orchard)

### 4.1 Stated assumptions

- Purchase of **4,000 m² of 畑 at ¥920/m²** (= the Fukuoka average of ¥920,000/10a). Individual plots vary widely; treat the land line as the model's biggest single uncertainty.
- Planting per the corrected spacing: **mikan 125 trees** (4 m × 4 m on 0.2 ha), **peach 40** and **pear 40** (5 m × 5 m on 0.1 ha each).
- Yield ramp per tree (kg), consistent with the fruit-tree guides in this repo (flowers removed in the establishment phase; **no mikan crop until Year 3**):

| Crop | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 | Y4 | Y5 | Mature |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Peach (per tree) | 0 | 3 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 |

- Blended prices per §3. Own and household labour is unpaid (side business); casual hired help at harvest is budgeted from Year 3.

**Reading the model honestly:**

- **Year 1 revenue is ¥0.** Newly planted trees are cropped off to establish. The old plan's "Year-1 revenue ¥1.1–1.8M" was impossible on the pack's own agronomy.
- The orchard covers its **annual running costs from Year 3** on these assumptions.
- At maturity (Year 8+), the same assumptions give ~¥5.7M/year gross revenue against ~¥0.75M running costs, which would clear the remaining cumulative deficit around **Year 7–8**. Treat that as an illustration of the shape, not a promise: it depends on prices holding, yields being achieved on weekend labour, and no catastrophic season.
- Unpaid own labour is the hidden subsidy in every small-orchard model, this one included. The plan works as a side business because the salary carries the household; the orchard must never be presented — to the committee or to ourselves — as if it yet does.
- Sensitivity: at ±30% on prices, Year-5 revenue ranges ~¥2.3M–4.3M; the Year-3 operating break-even is the assumption most exposed to a bad price or yield year.

## 5. Funding the purchase

- Base case: **self-funded from savings/salary**, given §2. This is also the simplest story at committee interview:資金繰り (funding) is a stated item in the application annex.
- If borrowing were needed: ordinary JFC agricultural loans and JA loans exist at low policy rates, but the 0% 青年等就農資金 is certification-gated and excludes land. Do not write any loan into the application unless it is actually arranged — the form asks for funding that is *certain*, not hoped for.
