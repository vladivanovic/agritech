# FACT-CHECK REPORT — Agritech Research Pack

**Checked:** 30 October 2026
**Scope:** All 16 Markdown files in this repository (GitHub: vladivanovic/agritech, local clone).
**Method:** Material claims extracted from the docs and checked against official sources — MAFF （農林水産省）, Munakata City, Fukuoka Prefecture, MLIT, NTA/METI, JFC, JA Group, municipal market statistics. The original documents were **not** edited.
**Verdicts:** ✅ Confirmed · 🕐 Outdated · ❌ Wrong · ❓ Unverifiable

---

## Executive summary

The pack reads fluently but is **not safe to rely on or submit in its current state**. The crop-growing guides are mostly generic-but-serviceable agronomy. The damage is concentrated exactly where it matters most: the legal procedure, the subsidy/finance figures, the contact directory, the entire river-water section, and the headline numbers in the management plan.

| Verdict | Count |
|---|---|
| ✅ Confirmed | 11 |
| 🕐 Outdated | 3 |
| ❌ Wrong | 45 |
| ❓ Unverifiable | 7 |
| **Total claims checked** | **66** |

Headline problems:

1. **The "50 ares minimum" (下限面積） no longer exists** — abolished 1 April 2023 — yet it appears as a live permission criterion in two documents, and the whole 0.4 ha plan is sized against a repealed rule.
2. **Article 3 permission is granted by the *municipal* Agricultural Committee （宗像市農業委員会）, not a "Prefectural Agricultural Committee"**, and the 農地法 imposes **no nationality or residence-status eligibility rule** — the docs' "permanent residents only / work-visa holders NOT eligible" framing is wrong.
3. **Every phone number in legal-considerations.md is wrong**, including a Munakata number with a Fukuoka City (092) area code. The real Munakata Agricultural Committee secretariat is **0940-36-0046**.
4. **The subsidy section of the assessment is fabricated** ("Foreign Farmer Training grants up to ¥2M", "6-week course with ¥1M support" — no such programs). The real support stack （経営開始資金 ¥1.65M/yr × 3 yrs, 経営発展支援事業， interest-free 青年等就農資金 up to ¥37M) is *more* generous than the docs claim, but works completely differently.
5. **Section 5 of legal-considerations.md (river water rights) is invented end-to-end** — wrong river name, wrong office, invented fee tables and process. Munakata's river is the **釣川 (Tsurikawa)**, a prefecture-managed Class-2 river; water use is governed by River Act Art. 23 permission and, in practice, the local land improvement district.
6. **The management plan's finances do not survive arithmetic**: its own tables don't sum to its stated totals, it charges fixed-asset tax on land it also rents, assumes ¥1.1–1.8M of revenue in the planting year from trees its own guides say bear nothing in Year 1, and prices mikan at 3–8× real market levels.
7. **Two "mandatory licences" do not exist** (the MAFF pesticide licence and the prefectural "Tractor Driver Qualification"), and farmland rents and walk-behind tractor prices are overstated by roughly an order of magnitude.

What is genuinely good: the overall shape of the Article 3 process, the 150-day engagement standard, the existence and roles of JAむなかた / むなかた地域農業活性化機構 / 道の駅むなかた / 北筑前普及指導センター， the farmland *purchase* price brackets, per-tree mature yields, and the food-processing licence/HACCP framework are all basically right.
