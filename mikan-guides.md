## 1. Mandarin/Mikan (Citrus unshiu)

### 1.1 Climate Suitability
- **Rating**: ★★★★★ (Best for Munakata, Fukuoka)
- **Temperature Range**: 15-28°C optimal; tolerates 0-35°C but growth slows outside 10-30°C
- **Rainfall**: 1,500-2,000mm annually; Munakata receives ~1,800mm - suitable
- **Hardiness Zone**: Zone 9a-9b; Munakata is Zone 9b-10a
- **Chill Hours**: Requires 200-400 hours below 7°C for flower bud differentiation; Munakata has mild winters (sufficient)

### 1.2 Recommended Varieties for Munakata
| Variety | Characteristics | Harvest Season | Notes |
|---------|----------------|----------------|-------|
| **Mikan (Standard)** | Sweet, easy-peel, standard size | Nov-Jan | Most common; prefecture specialty |
| **Harumikan** | Early ripening, high quality | Mar-Apr | Premium; good for early market |
| **Kiyomi** | Large fruit, less acidity, aromatic | Dec-Jan | Cross between mikan & orange |
| **Asami** | High sugar, very juicy | Feb-Mar | Premium variety; limited availability |

### 1.3 Planting & Care

**Planting Timeline:**
- **Optimal**: Dormant season Nov-Feb
- **Avoid**: Off-season (flowering/fruiting periods)
- **Seedling age**: 1-year or 2-year-old branches recommended

**Planting Procedure:**
1. **Hole preparation**: 50cm×50cm×50cm hole
2. **Soil mix**: 60% native soil + 30% compost + 10% river sand for drainage
3. **Planting depth**: Root ball top level with ground surface
4. **Watering**: Thorough watering after planting; 30L per seedling
5. **Mulching**: straw or wood chips 5-8cm depth around base

**Annual Care Calendar:**
| Month | Task |
|-------|------|
| **Nov-Feb** | Planting, base fertilizer (compost), mulching |
| **Mar** | Spring top-dressing (nitrogen促进 shoot growth) |
| **Apr-May** | Flower thinning (remove weak flowers), pest monitoring |
| **Jun** | Summer care, ensure water supply, bag fruit if desired |
| **Jul-Aug** | Fruit enlargement, bagging (optional for mikan), pest control |
| **Sep-Oct** | Early varieties harvest begin, leaf removal for sun exposure |
| **Nov** | Harvest late varieties, post-harvest fertilizer application |

**Fertilization Schedule:**
- **Base fertilizer** (planting): Organic compost 10-20kg/m²
- **Spring** (Mar): Nitrogen-rich fertilizer 50-100g N per tree
- **Summer** (Jun-Jul): Balanced N-P-K 100-150g per tree
- **Post-harvest** (Nov): Potassium-rich fertilizer 100-150g per tree

### 1.4 Growth Patterns
- **Year 1**: Root establishment; minimal harvesting (remove flowers/fruit)
- **Year 2-3**: First light harvest; tree structure development
- **Year 4-5**: Full production; 50-80kg per tree annually
- **Mature (Year 6+)**: 80-150kg per tree; peak production

### 1.5 Protection & Pest Management

**Common Pests:**
- **Citrus leafminer**: Larvae mine leaves; control with neem oil or spinosad
- **Asian citrus psyllid**: Vector of Huangbingbing disease; monitor with yellow sticky traps
- **Aphids**: New shoot infestation; control with insecticidal soap
- **Spider mites**: Dry condition proliferation; maintain humidity, water spraying

**Common Diseases:**
- **Citrus canker**: Bacterial; remove infected branches, copper fungicide spray
- **Brown rot**: Fungal; improve air circulation, remove mummified fruit
- **Root rot (Phytophthora)**: Overwatering; ensure drainage, avoid wet conditions

**Protection Methods:**
- **Bagging**: Individual fruit bags (paper or nylon) prevent pest damage and sunburn
- **Shoji nets**: 3mm mesh over orchard excludes birds and larger pests
- **Band trapping**: Sticky bands around trunk prevent ant climbing
- **Habitat management**: Maintain ground cover to beneficial insects

### 1.6 Harvest & Post-Harvest
- **Harvest indicators**: Color (deep orange-yellow), ease of separation from tree
- **Season**: Main crop Nov-Jan; early varieties Oct; late varieties Feb
- **Storage**: 1-2 months at 7-10°C, 90-95% humidity
- **Post-harvest care**: Remove stems, gentle handling, quick cooling
