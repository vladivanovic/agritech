# Comprehensive Greenhouse Design Guide for Munakata, Fukuoka

## 1. Greenhouse Types and Selection Criteria

### 1.1 Greenhouse Type Comparison

| Type | Structure | Cost (¥) | Cost (USD) | Best For | Climate Control |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|----------|-----------------|
| **Polytunnel** | Plastic film over hoops | ¥3,000,000 - ¥6,000,000 | $21,000 - $43,000 | Vegetables (beans, cucumbers, tomatoes) | Basic (roll-up sides) |
| **Polycarbonate** | Aluminum frame + panels | ¥8,000,000 - ¥15,000,000 | $57,000 - $107,000 | Tomatoes, melons, year-round | Excellent (insulated) |
| **Glass greenhouse** | Glass panels + metal frame | ¥15,000,000 - ¥30,000,000 | $107,000 - $210,000 | Research, high-value crops | Superior (light transmission) |
| **Simple house** | Bamboo + plastic sheeting | ¥500,000 - ¥1,500,000 | $3,500 - $10,500 | Seedlings, leafy greens | Minimal |

### 1.2 Recommended for Munakata Climate
- **Primary**: Polycarbonate greenhouse (best balance of climate control and cost)
- **Secondary**: Polytunnel (for seasonal vegetable production)
- **Tertiary**: Simple house (for seedlings, start-up phase)

## 2. Common Greenhouse Design Elements

### 2.1 Structural Components

| Component | Specification | Materials | Cost Range |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| **Frame** | 2.5-3.5m height | Galvanized steel / aluminum | ¥1,500,000 - ¥4,000,000 |
| **Roof** | 20°-30° slope | Polycarbonate panels (6mm, UV-coated) | ¥500,000 - ¥1,500,000 |
| **Sidewalls** | 1.5-2.0m height | Polycarbonate / polyethylene film | ¥300,000 - ¥1,000,000 |
| **Flooring** | Concrete or compacted soil | Cement or compacted ground | ¥200,000 - ¥800,000 |
| **Anchoring** | Ground spikes / concrete base | Steel pipes, concrete | ¥100,000 - ¥300,000 |

### 2.2 Ventilation System (CRITICAL)
| Element | Specification | Purpose |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Roof vents** | 10-15% of roof area, automatic openers | Heat escape (hot air rises) |
| **Side vents** | 20-30% of sidewall area, manual/auto | Air circulation, humidity control |
| **Exhaust fans** | 2-3 units, 3,000-5,000 m³/h each | Forced air circulation |
| **Inlet vents** | Opposite exhaust, adjustable | Create negative pressure flow |
| **Circulation fans** | Horizontal airflow (HAF) fans | Prevent hot spots, uniform temp |

### 2.2.1 Ventilation Rate Requirements
| Crop | Air Exchanges/Hour | Notes |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| **Tomatoes** | 40-60 | Disease prevention critical |
| **Cucumbers** | 40-50 | High humidity risk |
| **Melons/Watermelons** | 30-40 | Heat tolerance needed |
| **Beans** | 20-30 | Moderate humidity |

### 2.3 Irrigation System
| Component | Specification | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Main line** | HDPE pipe, 25-32mm diameter | Water distribution |
| **Drip tubing** | 16mm with emitters | Water-efficient delivery |
| **Emitters** | 2-4 per plant, 2-4 L/hour | Consistent moisture |
| **Filter** | 150-mesh minimum | Prevent clogging |
| **Fertilizer injector** | Proportioner, 1-10% concentration | Fertigation |
| **Water source** | Municipal well, rainwater catchment | Supply reliability |

### 2.4 Temperature Control

## 3. Crop-Specific Greenhouse Design

### 3.1 Melons and Watermelons

#### 3.1.1 Design Specifications
- **Height**: 2.5-3.0m (vine training space)
- **Width**: 6-8m (allow vine spreading + access paths)
- **Length**: 20-30m (flexible planting density)
- **Plant density**: 2-3 melon plants per 10m², or 3-4 watermelon plants per 10m²
- **Training system**: Vertical on strings/trellis with fruit slings

#### 3.1.2 Ventilation Priority
- **Maximum temperature**: Must not exceed 32°C during day
- **Minimum temperature**: Maintain 15°C at night
- **Humidity**: 60-70% (prevents disease, aids pollination)
- **Ventilation rate**: 30-40 air exchanges/hour

#### 3.1.3 Special Features
- **Fruit slings**: Net or cloth to support developing melons (prevents ground rot, supports weight)
- **String training**: 2-3 main vines per plant, trained vertically
- **Pollination**: Bees introduced (2-3 hives per hectare) OR hand pollination (male flowers, transfer pollen to female)
- **Reflective mulch**: Silver or white plastic under plants (reflects light, reduces disease, moderates soil temp)

### 3.2 Tomatoes

#### 3.2.1 Design Specifications
- **Height**: 2.5-3.5m (indeterminate types will grow tall)
- **Width**: 6-8m (2-3 rows + aisles)
- **Length**: 30-50m (depends on scale)
- **Plant density**: 2 rows per bay, 50cm between plants = ~4 plants per 10m² bay
- **Training system**: Single main stem to overhead string (pendant system)

#### 3.2.2 Ventilation Priority
- **Maximum temperature**: 30°C (above this, fruit quality declines)
- **Minimum temperature**: 10°C (below this, growth stops)
- **Humidity**: 60-80% (prevents fungal diseases)
- **Ventilation rate**: 40-60 air exchanges/hour

#### 3.2.2 Special Features
- **Pendant system**: String hangs from overhead rail, plant trained up to it
- **Leaf pruning**: Remove lower leaves as plant grows (improves airflow, reduces disease)
- **Side shoot removal**: Weekly, to maintain single stem
- **Bumblebees**: 1 hive per 1,000 m² for pollination (essential for fruit set)
- **Climate computers**: Automated ventilation, heating, cooling control (highly recommended for year-round)

### 3.3 Beans

#### 3.3.1 Design Specifications
- **Height**: 2.0-2.5m (pole beans need support)
- **Width**: 4-6m (1-2 rows, easy access)
- **Length**: 10-20m (smaller scale, intercrop possible)
- **Plant density**: 
  - Pole beans: 3-4 plants per 30cm = ~12-16 plants per m²
  - Bush beans: 10cm between plants = ~100 plants per m²
- **Training system**: 
  - Pole beans: Strings or netting at 2m height
  - Bush beans: No training needed, compact growth

#### 3.3.2 Ventilation Priority
- **Maximum temperature**: 30°C (avoid heat stress during flowering)
- **Minimum temperature**: 15°C (beans are warm-season but not frost tolerant)
- **Humidity**: 50-60% (prevents powdery mildew)
- **Ventilation rate**: 20-30 air exchanges/hour

#### 3.3.3 Special Features
- **Netting/trellis**: 2m height, installed at planting
- **No pollination needed** (self-pollinating)
- **Crop rotation**: Excellent preceding crop (fixes nitrogen); follow with tomatoes, tomatoes, or leafy greens
- **Row covers**: Early season (remove at flowering for pollination)

### 3.4 Cucumbers

#### 3.4.1 Design Specifications
- **Height**: 2.5-3.0m (vines need vertical space)
- **Width**: 6-8m (2-3 rows + aisles)
- **Length**: 30-50m (long season crop)
- **Plant density**: 2 rows per bay, 40-50cm between plants = ~4-6 plants per m²
- **Training system**: Vertical on strings/netting (essential)

#### 3.4.2 Ventilation Priority
- **Maximum temperature**: 35°C (cuke tolerate heat but monitor)
- **Minimum temperature**: 15°C (below slows growth)
- **Humidity**: 60-70% (critical - mildew risk above 80%)
- **Ventilation rate**: 40-50 air exchanges/hour

#### 3.4.3 Special Features
- **Vertical training**: Essential - vines climb strings from base to overhead
- **Parthenocarpic varieties**: Female-only flowers for indoor conditions (no pollination needed)
- **Leaf pruning**: Remove lower leaves (ground level) as plant grows (1-2 leaves/week)
- **Male flower removal**: If not using parthenocarpic types, remove male flowers to prevent bitterness
- **Pollination**: If not parthenocarpic, introduce bees (1 hive per 500 m²) or hand pollinate

## 5. Construction and Installation Timeline

### 5.1 Timeline (30×10m Polycarbonate Greenhouse)
| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Planning & Permits** | 1-2 months | Site selection, design, Agricultural Committee consultation |
| **Foundation** | 2-3 weeks | Land leveling, concrete perimeter, anchoring points |
| **Frame Assembly** | 2-3 weeks | Structural erection, door/window installation |
| **Cover Installation** | 1-2 weeks | Panel/film installation, sealing |
| **Systems Installation** | 2-3 weeks | Ventilation, irrigation, heating, cooling, electrical |
| **Testing & Commissioning** | 1 week | Ventilation checks, irrigation test, climate control setup |
| **Total** | **2-3 months** | From planning to operational |

### 5.2 Key Success Factors
- **Site selection**: Good drainage, wind protection, power/water access, south-facing slope optimal
- **Foundation**: Critical for longevity; uneven ground causes structural stress
- **Ventilation**: Oversize rather than undersize; humidity control = disease prevention
- **Quality materials**: UV-coated panels, galvanized steel (10+ year lifespan)
- **Automation**: Climate computers pay off in year-round production scenarios
