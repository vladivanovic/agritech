# 宗像市 農地法第3条許可申請 — 提出書類ドラフト
# Munakata City — Article 3 Permission Application: Submission Draft

> **⚠️ This is a DRAFT prepared to Munakata City's published format — it is not the official form and must not be submitted as-is.**
>
> - Download the **official form files** from the city (links in §1) and transfer the content onto them, by hand or in Word, **together with the Agricultural Committee secretariat**.
> - Every personal field is a **fill-in placeholder**, written as ［ 項目：記入 ］. This file intentionally contains **no personal data** — no names, no home address, no dates of birth, no family details, no residence-card numbers. Complete those offline, on the official forms, at filing time.
> - Draft content describing the *farming scenario* (an individual buying farmland to plant a fruit orchard as a side business) is pre-written where the forms ask for it. **Check every pre-written statement against your real facts before using it** — in particular the farm-work experience and the annual farm-work days (§5.2, §7.2). The committee interviews applicants; anything written must be true.
> - Structure, item order and wording follow the city's own forms and filled example as published in October 2026.
