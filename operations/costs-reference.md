# Costs Reference — Corrected Price Levels

*General price reference for planning. The project-specific budget is in [../03-finance-and-subsidies.md](../03-finance-and-subsidies.md). Corrected October 2026 per [FACTCHECK.md](../FACTCHECK.md) #34, #45–48, #62: the original version overstated rents ~10–20×, machinery ~10×, and farmland fixed asset tax ~25–50×, and mixed two different acquisition taxes together.*

All figures yen. Treat every range as a planning bracket to be replaced by local quotes, not as a quote itself.

### 1.3 Acquisition taxes and fees on a purchase

These are **two different taxes** (the original document merged them into a fictional "4–6% registration/transfer tax paid to the Legal Affairs Bureau"):

| Item | Basis | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 不動産取得税 (real-estate acquisition tax) | **3% of the assessed value** for land (reduced rate to Mar 2027) — a **prefectural** tax | A 1/3 base deduction applies to farmland acquired under a 農用地利用集積等促進計画； other reductions may apply — confirm with the prefectural tax office |
| 登録免許税 (registration licence tax) | Levied on the assessed value; standard 2% for ownership transfer, with agricultural relief measures | Paid at registration via the Legal Affairs Bureau process, usually through a 司法書士 |
| 司法書士 fee | ~¥60,000–150,000 | Market rate; obtain a quote |
| Boundary survey (if needed) | ¥200,000–500,000 | Only if boundaries are undetermined |

### 1.4 Holding cost

| Item | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 固定資産税 (fixed asset tax) | 1.4% of assessed value — but farmland is assessed at **agricultural-use value**, so for 0.4 ha expect **a few ¥10,000/year at most** (~¥20,000 used in the model) | The original pack's ¥1.0–1.8M/year was wrong by ~two orders of magnitude |
| 都市計画税 | Generally **not applicable** to rural farmland outside urbanisation promotion areas | The 0.3% line in the original doc does not apply here |

## 4. Greenhouse (reference — NOT in the core plan)

The original pack contradicted itself on its biggest capital item: a 20 m × 10 m polytunnel was priced ¥3–6M in two documents while the design document's own itemised build totalled ¥1.2–2.3M (FACTCHECK.md #62). Neither figure was validated against a builder's quote. **Quarantined position:** use the itemised build-up from [../reference/greenhouse-design.md](../reference/greenhouse-design.md) (≈¥1.2–2.3M for a basic polytunnel of that size, more for polycarbonate) as a *draft bracket only*, and **get a JA/builder estimate before budgeting anything**.

## 5. Annual operating benchmarks (0.4 ha orchard)

See the year-by-year table in the finance doc. Rules of thumb from that model: inputs (fertiliser + crop protection) ¥140,000–220,000/yr; fuel/utilities ¥60,000–100,000/yr; insurance ¥30,000–50,000/yr; packing/shipping and sales fees grow with volume. Part-time casual labour in Fukuoka is hired at roughly the prefectural minimum-wage level (per hour) plus transport — budget harvest help by the day actually needed, as the model does from Year 3.

## 6. Funding sources (summary)

Real programs only — full detail and the eligibility warning in the finance doc:

- 経営開始資金 ¥1.65M/yr × up to 3 yrs (income support; certification-gated)
- 経営発展支援事業 capital subsidy up to 3/4 of a project capped at ¥10M/¥5M (certification-gated)
- 青年等就農資金 0% loan up to ¥37M (certification-gated; land excluded)
- Ordinary JFC/JA agricultural loans at low policy rates (not the fabricated "1.5–3.0% new farmer loan ¥50M" of the original doc)
