## 2. Licences and qualifications — what is actually true

### 2.1 Things the original document claimed that **do not exist**

| Claimed licence | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Tractor Driver Qualification （耕運機・運転免許）" — prefecture-issued, 1-day course ¥30,000–50,000, 5-year renewal, "mandatory for public roads" | **There is no such licence.** See §2.2 for the real driving-licence rules |
| "Pesticide Handling Responsible Person （農薬取扱責任者）" — issued by MAFF, mandatory for any pesticide application, with an online exam | **Fabricated.** No national licence is needed to buy or apply ordinary pesticides on your own farm. The real (voluntary) credential is the prefectural **農薬指導士**, aimed at advisory/sales roles — see §2.4 |

### 2.2 Driving tractors on public roads — the real rules

Road use is governed by the ordinary driving-licence system:

- A tractor within the **small special vehicle （小型特殊自動車）** limits (length ≤4.7 m, width ≤1.7 m, height ≤2.0 m (2.8 m in specified cases), max speed ≤15 km/h) may be driven on public roads with an **ordinary licence （普通免許）** or a small-special-vehicle licence. Most 管理機-class and small farm tractors fall here.
- Anything bigger/faster needs a **大型特殊免許** (a farm-machinery-limited version exists).
- Driving on your own land needs no licence at all.
- Road-going farm vehicles also need number plates and, where applicable, 自賠責 insurance — ask the dealer or city office when buying.

### 2.3 Hazardous Materials Handler （危険物取扱者） — corrected facts

- The licence has **no expiry** (only a photo rewrite every 10 years). The exam is run by the **消防試験研究センター**, not issued by a fire station.
- Fire Service Act facility regulation applies at the **designated quantities （指定数量）**: **200 L for gasoline, 1,000 L for kerosene/diesel**. A small farm fuel store below those thresholds is governed by municipal small-quantity dangerous-goods rules — **not** by a licensing requirement. The original claim ("licence required if storing >10 L, 5-year renewal, issued by the Fire Department") was wrong on all three points.
- Practical guidance: keep farm fuel stocks small, in approved containers, away from buildings; ask the local fire headquarters if a planned store approaches the thresholds.

### 2.4 Real, relevant qualifications and permissions

| Item | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 農薬指導士 (Fukuoka Prefecture) | Voluntary | Prefectural certification for people in advisory/sales roles; not a condition of spraying your own crop. Your obligations as a grower are **label compliance and safe use under the 農薬取締法**, plus keeping application records |
| Food business permit （食品衛生法） | Required **only if processing food** (jam, juice…) | Applied for at the prefectural 保健福祉（環境）事務所； permit fee roughly **¥16,000** (manufacturing categories ~¥14,000–26,000), **no annual fee**, validity 5+ years. Facility fit-out is a separate (construction) cost. See the legal doc §4 |
| Chainsaw / forklift special training （特別教育） | Employment law | These obligations attach to **employers/employees** under the Industrial Safety and Health Act. A self-employed sole proprietor using his own machines is in a different, largely unregulated position — but training is still strongly recommended on safety grounds |
| 日本農業技術検定 | Voluntary | A real national skills test run by the 全国農業会議所. (The original doc's "Japan Agricultural Competency Test (JACT) by JA Zenchu" could not be found and has been removed) |
| 狩猟免許 / わな猟免許 | Only if trapping boar yourself | See [pest-and-wildlife.md](pest-and-wildlife.md) |

## 3. Funding for equipment

The fabricated subsidy table that stood here ("New Farmer Establishment Subsidy ¥10M", "Greenhouse Installation Subsidy ¥5M", "Irrigation Infrastructure Subsidy ¥3M", "Tractor/Diesel Equipment Subsidy ¥2M", "Safety Equipment Subsidy ¥500k") has been deleted in full — none of those programs exists. Real capital support for a certified new farmer is **経営発展支援事業** (project cap ¥10M / ¥5M, up to 3/4 of cost), and the real JFC product is **青年等就農資金** (0%, up to ¥37M, land purchase excluded) — both gated on 認定新規就農者 certification, which this side-business plan likely cannot obtain. The full, honest picture is in [../03-finance-and-subsidies.md](../03-finance-and-subsidies.md). Budget equipment as self-funded.
