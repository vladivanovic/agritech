# Greenhouse Guide: Cucumbers

## 1. Climate Suitability
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆ (High - greenhouse staple crop)
- **Optimal temperature**: 25-30°C day, 18-20°C night
- **Hardiness Zone**: Zone 8b-10a (Munakata excellent)
- **Growing season**: March-November (long season, frost-sensitive)
- **Day length**: 12-16 hours; uninterrupted night critical for flowering

## 2. Varieties Suitable for Greenhouse Cultivation

| Type | Variety | Characteristics | Harvest Period | Notes |
|------|---------|----------------|----------------|-------|
| **Slicing cucumbers** | Summer Glory | 20-25cm, dark green, crisp | May-October | Most common greenhouse variety |

## 3. Greenhouse Design for Cucumbers

### 3.1 Required Specifications
| Specification | Requirement | Purpose |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| **Height** | 2.5-3.0m | Vine training space |
| **Width** | 6-8m | 2-3 rows, aisles |
| **Length** | 30-50m | Flexible, depends on scale |
| **Ventilation** | 40-50 air exchanges/hour | Critical (cucumbers love heat + airflow) |
| **Temperature control** | 15-35°C range | Heat lovers but monitor extremes |

```
Planting Configuration:
- 2 rows per bay, 40-50cm between plants
- Vertical training on strings/netting (essential)
- Parthenocarpic (female-only) varieties for indoor conditions
- Aisle: 1.5m between rows for access

Vine Training (Essential):
- Vertical string/training from base to overhead rail
- Remove lateral shoots after 1st female flower
- Train 1-2 main vines per plant
- Pinch growing tip at desired height
- Leaf pruning: remove lower leaves (ground level) as plant grows
  - Improves airflow, reduces disease
  - Remove 1-2 leaves/week as plant grows
```

### 3.3 Irrigation & Fertilization
- **Drip irrigation**: 1 emitter per plant (or every 50cm along row)
- **Fertigation schedule**:
  - Transplanting to flowering: Balanced N-P-K
  - Flowering to fruit set: Potassium-heavy
  - Fruit enlargement: Consistent feeding
- **EC management**: 1.8-2.5 dS/m
- **pH management**: 5.8-6.2 optimal
- **Calcium**: Prevents misshapen fruit; apply via irrigation

### 3.4 Pest & Disease Management
- **Major diseases**: Powdery mildew, downy mildew, angular leaf spot, root rot, cucumber mosaic virus
- **Major pests**: Aphids, spider mites, whitefly, thrips, cucumber beetles
- **Prevention**:
  - 60-70% humidity management (critical - mildew risk)
  - Excellent ventilation (horizontal airflow essential)
  - Row covers early season (remove at flowering)
  - Yellow/blue sticky traps
  - Resistant varieties when possible
  - Remove symptomatic plants immediately
- **Organic options**: Neem oil, potassium bicarbonate, Bacillus subtilis, predatory mites

### 3.5 Harvest & Post-Harvest
- **Harvest indicators**: 
  - Size: 20-25cm for slicing, 8-10cm for mini
  - Color: Dark green, uniform
  - Firmness: Firm but not hard
- **Frequency**: Every 2-3 days during peak season
- **Yield**: 3-5kg per m² per season
- **Storage**: 1-2 weeks at 10-12°C, 90-95% humidity (NOT refrigerate below 10°C causes chilling injury)
- **Note**: Harvest early morning for best quality; frequent harvesting encourages more production
