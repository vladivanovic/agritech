# Agritech — Side-Business Fruit Growing in Munakata, Fukuoka

## What this project is

A plan to **buy a small plot of farmland in Munakata City, Fukuoka Prefecture, and grow fruit trees as a side business** alongside a full-time job. The core crop plan is an orchard of roughly **0.4 ha** — mikan (satsuma mandarin), peach and nashi pear — sold through local channels (JAむなかた, 道の駅むなかた, direct sales).

Buying farmland to farm it requires permission under **Article 3 of the Agricultural Land Act （農地法第3条）** from the **Munakata City Agricultural Committee （宗像市農業委員会）**. That permission — and honestly demonstrating that a full-time worker can still farm the land properly — is the centre of this project.

> **Status (October 2026):** Research phase. The original AI-drafted research pack was fact-checked against official sources (see [FACTCHECK.md](FACTCHECK.md): 66 claims checked, 45 wrong) and this repository has been restructured and corrected on the basis of that report. No land has been bought and nothing has been submitted to the city yet.

## The one thing to understand first

The Article 3 permission criteria include **常時従事** — the acquirer (or their household members) must be constantly engaged in the farm work the land needs, with **~150 days/year as the official working standard**. For someone working full-time, this is the key approval consideration. It is addressed head-on in [01-overview-and-strategy.md](01-overview-and-strategy.md) and in the draft submission document — not hidden, and with no invented workarounds.

## Privacy note

This is a **public repository**. It deliberately contains **no personal data**: no home address, no family members' names or ages, no dates of birth, no residence-card details. Where the city's forms require such information, the documents use fill-in placeholders to be completed offline at submission time.
