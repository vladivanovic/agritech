## 2. Required Licenses and Qualifications

### 2.1 Mandatory Licenses for New Farmers

| License/Qualification | Issuing Organization | Validity | Cost | Required For |
|----------------------|--------------------|----------|------|--------------|
| **Tractor Driver Qualification** | Prefecture Agricultural Hall | 5 years (renewal) | ¥30,000 - ¥50,000 | Operating tractor on public roads; recommended for all tractor users |
| **Hazardous Materials Handler** | Fukuoka Fire Department | 5 years (renewal) | ¥10,000 - ¥20,000 | Storing >10L fuel for pumps/heaters; applying certain pesticides |
| **Pesticide Handling Responsible Person** | MAFF / Prefecture | Indefinite | ¥5,000 - ¥10,000 | Any pesticide application; mandatory for commercial farming |
| **Plant House Management Specialist** | Relevant Association | Indefinite | ¥10,000 - ¥30,000 | Greenhouse operations >500m²; recommended not mandatory |

### 2.2 Optional but Highly Recommended

| License/Qualification | Issuing Organization | Cost | Benefits |
|----------------------|--------------------|------|----------|
| **Basic Agricultural Skills Specialist** | Agricultural School / Prefecture | ¥50,000 - ¥100,000 (course) | Comprehensive training: cultivation, machinery, business; valued by Agricultural Committee |
| **Food Business License** | Fukuoka Health Department | ¥100,000 - ¥200,000 (setup) + ¥20,000/year | REQUIRED for selling fruit jams, processed foods |
| **Japan Agricultural Competency Test (JACT)** | JA Zenchu | ¥6,000 - ¥12,000 | National certification; levels 1-3; valuable for subsidies and credibility |
| **Greenhouse Manager Certification** | Greenhouse Industry Assoc. | ¥20,000 - ¥50,000 | Specialized greenhouse operation techniques |

### 2.3 License Acquisition Process

#### 2.2.1 Tractor Driver Qualification
1. **Eligibility**: 18+ years, no criminal record, physically capable
2. **Training**: 
   - Classroom: Traffic laws, safety regulations (4 hours)
   - Practical: Tractor operation, safety maneuvers (6 hours)
3. **Exam**: Written test + practical operation test
4. **Issuance**: At passing; card mailed in 2 weeks
5. **Renewal**: Every 5 years; 1-day refresher course (¥15,000-25,000)

**Where to take**: Prefecture Agricultural Hall (福岡県農業会館), Munakata Satellite Center
**Contact**: 092-883-2227 (Fukuoka Prefecture Agriculture Department)

#### 2.2.2 Hazardous Materials Handler
1. **Eligibility**: 18+ years, no criminal record
2. **Training**:
   - Laws and regulations (2 hours)
   - Storage methods (3 hours)
   - Emergency response (2 hours)
   - Quiz (20 questions, 60% passing)
3. **Issuance**: Same day at passing
4. **Renewal**: Every 5 years; 3-hour course (¥10,000-20,000)

**Where to take**: Fukuoka Fire Department (福岡県消防本部), Munakata Fire Station
**Contact**: 094-031-0119 (Munakata Fire Station)

#### 2.2.3 Pesticide Handling Responsible Person
1. **Eligibility**: 18+ years, associated with farming operation
2. **Training**:
   - Online course (available in Japanese/English) or in-person
   - Pesticide laws, safety, application methods, environmental protection
3. **Exam**: 30-question test; 70% passing
4. **Issuance**: Certificate posted/emailed within 2 weeks
5. **Validity**: Indefinite; no renewal required

**Where to take**: MAFF website (online), or Prefecture Agriculture Department in-person
**Online**: www.maff.go.jp/pesticide/license (Japanese/English available)

#### 2.2.4 Food Business License (for processed foods)
1. **Facility requirements**: Separate kitchen, proper ventilation, stainless steel surfaces, hand-washing station
2. **Application**:
   - Submit to Fukuoka Health Department with facility plans
   - On-site inspection (2-4 weeks after submission)
   - ¥100,000-200,000 setup fee + ¥20,000/year renewal
3. **Requirements**:
   - 10m² minimum workspace
   - 5°C refrigeration
   - Hand-washing station with warm water
   - Insect-proof, dust-proof construction
   - Waste disposal plan

**Contact**: Fukuoka Prefecture Health Department (福岡県保健福祉部保健所)
**Phone**: 092-641-1211

## 4. License & Equipment Checklist for New Farmers

### 4.1 Pre-Farming (Before Planting)
- [ ] **Agricultural Land Law permission** (Article 3) - obtained
- [ ] **Tractor Driver Qualification** - completed (or plan to take within 3 months)
- [ ] **Pesticide Handling Responsible Person** - certified
- [ ] **Hazardous Materials Handler** - if storing fuel (fuel >10L)
- [ ] **Basic Agricultural Skills course** - recommended (JA offering)
- [ ] **Fencing installed** (boar prevention, see separate guide)
- [ ] **Irrigation system** operational and tested
- [ ] **Tool inventory** purchased; basic maintenance supplies

### 4.2 First Year (Farming Year 1)
- [ ] **License renewals** tracked (Tractor: 5-year; HazMat: 5-year)
- [ ] **Pesticide records** maintained (required for inspection)
- [ ] **Equipment maintenance schedule** established
- [ ] **Safety equipment** available: first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, PPE
- [ ] **Insurance**: Crop insurance, liability insurance (consider)

### 4.3 Annual Maintenance
| Task | Frequency | Responsibility |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Tractor inspection/service | Annual (before season) | Owner or mechanic |
| License renewal | As per schedule (see above) | Owner/administrative lawyer |
| Tool sharpening/sharpening | As needed (every 2-3 years for major tools) | Owner or tool specialist |
| Safety equipment check | Seasonal (before each main season) | Owner |
| Pesticide license update | Every 5 years (HazMat) or indefinite (Pesticide) | Owner |

### 1.3 Bore Pump (Groundwater Pump)
| Specification | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Type** | Submersible bore pump or centrifugal bore pump |
| **Typical depth** | 30-100m (depends on aquifer table; Munakata average 40-60m) |
| **Flow rate** | 10-50 L/min for agricultural use; 50-100 L/min for larger farms |
| **Power** | 3-7HP electric motor; diesel option for remote locations without electricity |
| **Cost (¥)** | ¥200,000 - ¥800,000 (unit only; installation extra ¥100,000-¥300,000) |
| **Cost (USD)** | $1,400 - $5,600 |
| **Installation** | Requires licensed well driller; electrician for power connection |

### Bore Pump Application Process

| Step | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 1. Site Survey | Hydrogeological survey or test well drilling | Determine aquifer depth, quality, yield |
| 2. Permit Application | Munakata City Water Department | May require permission for well drilling |
| 3. Contractor Selection | Licensed well driller | Get multiple quotes |
| 4. Installation | Drill well, install pump, electrical connection | 1-2 weeks typical |
| 5. Testing | Flow test, water quality test | Ensure adequate supply and potability |
| 6. Registration | Register with local water authority | Required for agricultural use |

### Bore Pump Selection Guide
| Crop Type | Recommended Flow | Pressure | Power |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|-------|
| Small orchard (0.5 ha) | 10-15 L/min | 20-30 m head | 3-5HP electric |
| Medium farm (1-2 ha) | 20-30 L/min | 30-50 m head | 5-7HP electric/diesel |
| Large farm (2+ ha) | 40-60 L/min | 50-80 m head | 7-10HP diesel (remote) |

### Maintenance
- Annual inspection of pump and motor
- Clean intake screen quarterly
- Check pressure gauge and flow meter
- Winterize if freezing risk (drain or antifreeze)
- Professional service every 2-3 years (¥30,000-¥50,000)
