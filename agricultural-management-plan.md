### Risk Management

#### Identified Risks & Mitigation
| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **Frost damage (peach blossom)** | High (Mar) | Crop loss 30-100% | Orchard fans, heaters, sprinkler system; select frost-resistant varieties (Rising Sun early harvest) |
| **Wild boar damage** | Medium-High | 10-30% crop loss | 1.5m fencing + electric strands (see pest prevention guide); ¥150k-350k investment |
| **Unfavorable weather (typhoon)** | Medium | Fruit damage, tree loss | Windbreaks, insurance, harvest early if storm predicted |
| **Pest & disease outbreaks** | Medium | Reduced yield/quality | Integrated pest management, regular monitoring, resistant varieties, sanitation |
| **Market price fluctuations** | Medium | Reduced income | Diversify sales channels, direct consumer relationships, value-added processing |
| **Water shortage** | Low-Medium | Reduced growth/yield | Water conservation techniques, mulching, drought-resistant varieties, efficient irrigation |
| **Labor shortages** | Low | Delayed operations | Family labor, part-time workers, mechanization where possible, community exchange |

### Application Flow (5 Steps)
1. **Land search & owner discussion**
2. **Pre-consultation with Agricultural Committee**
3. **Document preparation & submission**
4. **Document review & on-site inspection**
5. **Committee meeting decision** (≈4 weeks processing time)

### Judgment Criteria (Agricultural Committee)
- All-efficient-use requirement
- Permanent engagement in farming (150+ days/year)
- Business scale (50+ ares in prefectures)
- No hindrance to surrounding agriculture
- Nationality/residency status compliance

### Common Mistakes & Countermeasures
- **Unrealistic numbers** → Collect local JA guidance, on-site inspection data
- **Poorly written plan** → Get revisions from senior farmers or advisors
- **Missing/expired documents** → Create checklist; only documents within 3 months valid
- **Unable to attend on-site inspection** → Use agent (administrative lawyer, etc.), explain via video
- **Residency document mistakes** → Confirm with Immigration Bureau, check expiry validity
