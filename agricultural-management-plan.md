## 2. Farming Plan Overview

### 2.1 Goal Statement
Transition from urban to rural agriculture in Munakata, Fukuoka, producing high-quality citrus (Mikan) and stone fruits (Peach, Pear) for local markets and direct-to-consumer sales. Target: establish 0.5 ha of orchard in Year 1, expanding to 1.5 ha by Year 3.

### 2.2 Core Philosophy
- **Sustainable orchard cultivation**: Build soil health, conserve water, minimize chemical inputs
- **Quality over quantity**: Focus on premium varieties with Munakata branding
- **Community integration**: Participate in JA markets, local events, knowledge exchange with senior farmers
- **Long-term commitment**: Intend permanent engagement (150+ days/year as required by Agricultural Land Law)

- **Peach**: ¥1,500-2,500/fruit (200-300g); premium varieties ¥3,000-5,000

- **Packaging**: Simple cardboard boxes with farm name, crop type, harvest date
- **Traceability**: Record-keeping for each batch (variety, plot, harvest date)
- **Quality goals**: Standardize on >12 Brix for fruit sugars; uniform size/color
