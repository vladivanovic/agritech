# Fruit Tree Guide — Mikan （温州みかん, Satsuma Mandarin)

*One of three fruit-tree guides in this project; all three use the same structure. Planning figures match [03-finance-and-subsidies.md](../03-finance-and-subsidies.md).*

## Overview

- **Crop:** 温州みかん (*Citrus unshiu*), evergreen citrus.
- **Fit for Munakata:** ★★★★★ — the backbone crop of this plan. Munakata's mild winters (hardiness roughly Zone 9b) and ~1,800 mm annual rainfall suit satsuma well; Fukuoka is an established citrus prefecture.
- **In this plan:** 0.2 ha (2,000 m²), **125 trees at 4 m × 4 m spacing**.
- **Climate needs:** Optimum growth 15–28 °C; some winter chill aids flower-bud differentiation; trees are damaged by hard frosts below about −5 °C, so avoid frost-pocket sites.

## Varieties

| Variety | Harvest (Fukuoka) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 温州みかん standard strains （宮川早生 etc.) | Oct–Jan depending on strain (early strains Oct–Nov; regular Nov–Jan) | The reliable main crop; stagger strains to spread harvest labour |
| はるみ (Harumi) | Feb–Mar | Large, high-sugar dekopon-relative; extends the selling season |
| きよみ (Kiyomi) | Feb–Apr | Mikan × orange cross; aromatic, later season |
| Late/storage types | Dec–Jan, sold from storage | Smooths labour and cash flow |

Buy grafted nursery trees (1–2-year-old) from a reputable nursery; do not rely on informal variety names.

## Site & soil

- Full sun, sheltered from cold winter north-west wind; a gentle south-facing slope is ideal and drains cold air.
- Well-drained soil; citrus hates wet feet (root rot). Avoid heavy clay unless drainage is improved.
- Soil mix at planting (from the original guide): ~60% native soil + 30% compost + 10% coarse sand where drainage needs help.
- Access for a 管理機 and a small vehicle; water source for establishment irrigation.

## Planting

- **When:** Dormant season, Nov–Feb (spring planting Mar also used in Kyushu).
- **Hole:** ~50 cm × 50 cm × 50 cm.
- **Depth:** Root ball top level with the soil surface; graft union above soil.
- Water thoroughly at planting (~30 L/tree), mulch 5–8 cm (straw or wood chips), stake if exposed to wind.

## Growing & care

- **Fertilisation (guide):** base organic matter at planting; spring dressing (Mar, nitrogen for shoot growth); early-summer balanced feed (Jun–Jul); post-harvest feed (Nov, potassium-leaning). Established orchards in Japan typically fertilise in Mar, Jun/Jul and Oct/Nov — follow the extension centre's local schedule （北筑前普及指導センター）.
- **Water:** Regular watering in the first 2 summers; mature trees are fairly drought-tolerant but fruit enlargement (Jul–Sep) suffers in dry spells — drip irrigation pays off then.
- **Fruit thinning:** Remove excess fruitlets in summer to keep fruit size up and avoid biennial bearing; **remove all flowers/fruit in Years 1–2** so young trees build frame.
- **Ground management:** Mulch or managed grass cover; keep a vegetation-free ring around young trunks.

## Pruning & training

- Open, moderately-spreading form with 3–4 main scaffolds; aim for light into the canopy.
- Main prune late winter (Feb–Mar): remove dead, crossing and inward wood, water sprouts.
- Light summer tidy-up only; heavy summer pruning reduces next year's crop.

## Pest & disease management

| Threat | Notes / control |
|---|---|
| カイガラムシ (scale insects) | Dormant-season oil spray; prune for airflow |
| ハダニ (spider mites) | Worse in dry summers; monitor leaf undersides; registered miticides if needed |
| かいよう病 (citrus canker) | Bacterial; remove infected material, copper sprays at label timings, windbreaks reduce spread |
| アブラムシ / エカキムシ (aphids / leafminer) | Mainly on new flush; usually tolerable on mature trees |
| 根腐れ (Phytophthora root rot) | Drainage is the control — see Site & soil |

No licence is needed to buy or spray ordinary pesticides on your own crop — the obligations are label compliance and safe use under the 農薬取締法 (see [../operations/equipment-and-licences.md](../operations/equipment-and-licences.md)). Keep spray records.

## Harvest

- Pick by colour and taste, not colour alone; satsuma does not ripen off the tree.
- Season by strain: early Oct–Nov, regular Nov–Jan, late varieties into Feb–Apr.
- Storage: ~1–2 months at 5–10 °C with high humidity for regular strains.

## Risks & notes

- **Cold damage** is the standing climate risk in a cold snap; site selection is the mitigation.
- **Biennial bearing** if thinning is skipped — a labour-discipline issue for a weekend grower.
- Harvest (Oct–Jan) falls in a relatively manageable window for a side business, one reason mikan is the backbone crop here.
