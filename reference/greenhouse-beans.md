## 1. Climate suitability

- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆ (Moderate — warm-season crop, humidity sensitive)
- **Optimal temperature:** 20–25 °C day, 15–20 °C night
- **Season:** Warm season; frost-tender

## 2. Varieties suitable for greenhouse cultivation

| Type | Variety | Characteristics | Harvest period | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pole beans | Kintoki （金時） | Red skin, sweet, climbing | Jun–Aug | Traditional Japanese variety |

## 3. Greenhouse design for beans

### 3.1 Specifications

| Specification | Requirement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 2.0–2.5 m | Vine support (pole beans) |
| Width | 4–6 m | 1–2 rows, easy access |
| Length | 10–20 m | Flexible, small scale |
| Ventilation | 20–30 air exchanges/hour | Moderate |
| Temperature control | 15–30 °C range | Avoid heat stress |

```
Planting configuration:
- Pole beans: 3–4 seeds per hill, thinned to the 2–3 strongest plants; strings or netting to 2 m
- Bush beans: ~10 cm between plants, dense planting
- Aisle: 0.8 m between rows
```

Pole beans need support installed at planting; wind young vines gently around the string. No pruning — support vertical growth only.

### 3.3 Irrigation and fertilisation

- **Drip irrigation:** 1 emitter per plant (bush) or per hill (pole).
- **Fertilisation:** Beans are light feeders and fix nitrogen — avoid excess nitrogen (it suppresses flowering).
  - Base: organic compost 3–5 kg/m²
  - Side-dressing: phosphorus–potassium after flowering
- **EC:** 1.2–1.8 dS/m · **pH:** 6.0–6.8
- **Watering:** Consistent moisture through flowering and pod set; ease off slightly at maturity.

## 4. Pest and disease management

- **Major diseases:** Powdery mildew, bacterial blight, root rot (Phytophthora).
- **Major pests:** Aphids, bean beetles, spider mites.
- **Prevention:** 50–60% humidity management; good air circulation (avoid overcrowding); crop rotation (2+ years before beans again); disease-free seed; row covers early season (remove at flowering for pollination).
- **Lower-input options:** Neem oil, potassium bicarbonate, beneficial insects (ladybirds, parasitic wasps).

## 5. Harvest and post-harvest

- **Pole beans:** Harvest from ~60–70 days after planting; pick young pods at 10–15 cm.
- **Bush beans:** Harvest from ~50–60 days; pick regularly to keep plants producing.
- **Yield:** ~2–3 kg/m² pole beans; ~1.5–2 kg/m² bush beans.
- **Storage:** ~1 week refrigerated; blanch and freeze for longer storage.
- **Note:** Beans fix nitrogen — an excellent crop to plant *before* heavy feeders such as tomatoes in a rotation.
