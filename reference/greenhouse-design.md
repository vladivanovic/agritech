# Reference — Greenhouse Design Guide

*Secondary/reference material — a greenhouse is not part of the core orchard plan. Tidied October 2026. Cost figures are flagged in §4: the original pack contradicted itself on greenhouse costs by 2–3× (FACTCHECK.md #62) and nothing here has been validated against a builder's quote.*

## 1. Greenhouse types

| Type | Structure | Indicative cost (¥) | Best for | Climate control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Polytunnel （パイプハウス) | Plastic film over steel hoops | See §4 build-up | Seasonal vegetables | Basic (roll-up sides) |
| Polycarbonate house | Aluminium/steel frame + panels | See §4 build-up | Year-round, higher-value crops | Good insulation |
| Glass house | Glass panels + metal frame | Highest | Research, premium production | Best light transmission |
| Simple house | Bamboo + plastic sheeting | ~¥500,000–1,500,000 | Seedlings, start-up phase | Minimal |

For Munakata's climate (mild winters, typhoon exposure), a well-anchored polytunnel is the usual entry point; polycarbonate buys insulation and wind resistance at several times the price.

## 2. Common design elements

### 2.1 Structure

| Component | Specification | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | 2.5–3.5 m height | Galvanised steel / aluminium |
| Roof | 20–30° slope | Polycarbonate (6 mm, UV-coated) or film |
| Sidewalls | 1.5–2.0 m | Polycarbonate / polyethylene film |
| Flooring | Concrete or compacted soil | — |
| Anchoring | Ground screws / concrete base | Critical in typhoon country |

### 2.2 Ventilation (the critical system)

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Roof vents | 10–15% of roof area, automatic openers |
| Side vents | 20–30% of sidewall area |
| Exhaust fans | 2–3 units, 3,000–5,000 m³/h each |
| Circulation (HAF) fans | Prevent hot spots; uniform temperature |

Indicative ventilation rates: tomatoes 40–60 air changes/hour; cucumbers 40–50; melons 30–40; beans 20–30. Humidity control *is* disease control in a Japanese greenhouse.

### 2.3 Irrigation

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| Main line | HDPE pipe, 25–32 mm |
| Drip tubing | 16 mm with emitters, 2–4 L/hour |
| Filter | 150-mesh minimum |
| Fertiliser injector | Proportioner, 1–10% |
| Water source | Well, rainwater catchment, or district supply (subject to the water arrangements for the parcel) |

### 2.4 Temperature control

| System | Indicative cost (¥) |
|---|---|
| Heating (fuel heaters, thermostat) | ¥200,000–500,000 |
| Cooling (evaporative pads, fans) | ¥300,000–700,000 |
| Shading (screens/cloth) | ¥100,000–200,000 |
| Floor heating (seedlings; optional) | ¥500,000–1,500,000 |

## 3. Crop-specific notes

- **Tomatoes:** 2.5–3.5 m height for indeterminate vines trained to overhead strings; single-stem training, weekly side-shoot removal; bumblebees ~1 hive per 1,000 m²; max ~30 °C, humidity 60–80%.
- **Cucumbers:** Vertical strings essential; parthenocarpic varieties for indoor conditions; remove lower leaves progressively; max ~35 °C, humidity 60–70%.
- **Melons/watermelons:** Vertical training with fruit slings; bees or hand pollination; max ~32 °C day, ≥15 °C night.
- **Beans:** 2.0–2.5 m; netting/strings for pole types; self-pollinating; max ~30 °C.
- **Strawberries:** Elevated gutters 30–40 cm; concrete floor; humidity 60–70% (grey mould above 80%). Detail in [greenhouse-strawberry.md](greenhouse-strawberry.md).

## 5. Construction timeline (indicative, ~30 m × 10 m polycarbonate)

| Phase | Duration |
|---|---|
| Planning & consultations | 1–2 months |
| Foundation | 2–3 weeks |
| Frame assembly | 2–3 weeks |
| Cover installation | 1–2 weeks |
| Systems (ventilation, irrigation, heating, electrical) | 2–3 weeks |
| Testing & commissioning | 1 week |
| **Total** | **~2–3 months** |

Note: building a greenhouse on farmland may engage farmland/ building rules depending on structure and use — confirm with the Agricultural Committee secretariat and the city before assuming a house is a purely private construction decision.

## 6. Success factors

- Site: drainage, wind shelter, power/water access, southerly aspect.
- Foundation quality determines lifespan; uneven ground stresses the structure.
- Oversize ventilation rather than undersizing it.
- UV-coated covering and galvanised frames for a 10+ year life.
