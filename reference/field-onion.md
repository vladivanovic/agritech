# Reference — Field Guide: Onions

*Secondary/reference material — field vegetables are not part of the core orchard plan. Tidied October 2026; the water-requirement unit error corrected per FACTCHECK.md #59 (20–30 mm of water over 1 m² is 20–30 litres, not 200–300).*

## 1. Climate suitability

- **Rating:** ★★★★☆ for Munakata
- **Optimal temperature:** 13–24 °C; bulbing is triggered as day length exceeds ~12–14 hours
- **Season:** Plant Oct–Nov, harvest May–Jun (the Kyushu pattern)
- **Rainfall:** 1,000–1,500 mm adequate; drainage essential (onions rot in wet soil)

## 2. Varieties

Common Japanese types for autumn planting in Kyushu include early （早生）, mid-season and storage （晩生） varieties of yellow onion; sets (small bulbs) are the beginner-friendly option. Choose varieties by intended harvest month and storage need, and confirm current local recommendations with JA — variety line-ups change.

## 3. Planting

### 3.1 Preparation

- **Sets:** small dormant bulbs, 1–2 cm diameter; discard soft or damaged ones.
- **Seed:** direct-sow, or raise seedlings and transplant (the standard commercial route in Kyushu).

### 3.2 Field preparation

- **Soil:** well-drained loam or sandy loam, pH 6.0–6.8.
- **Beds:** raised beds 20–30 cm high, ~60 cm wide (wider multi-row beds are also common).
- **Spacing:** sets/seedlings 10–15 cm in-row, ~30 cm between rows.
- **Base fertiliser:** organic compost 5–10 kg/m² plus a balanced fertiliser per soil test.

### 3.3 Procedure

1. Form beds; black plastic mulch is optional (warms soil, suppresses weeds).
2. Plant sets 2–3 cm deep, pointed end up.
3. Water in (~30 L/m²) and keep moisture consistent while establishing.
4. Mulch (straw, 5 cm) to conserve moisture and suppress weeds.

## 4. Maintenance

### 4.1 Weed control

Hand-weed until the canopy closes (6–8 weeks); mulch or light inter-row cultivation thereafter.

### 4.2 Fertilisation schedule

- **Base:** compost 5–10 kg/m² + balanced N-P-K (e.g. 8-8-8) ~100 g/m².
- **Side-dress 1** (~3 weeks after planting): nitrogen ~30 g/m² for leaf growth.
- **Side-dress 2** (bulbing): potassium ~50 g/m²; reduce nitrogen.

### 4.3 Thinning

Thin direct-seeded rows to 10–15 cm spacing once seedlings are 10–15 cm tall.

## 5. Water requirements

- **Season total:** ~500–700 mm.
- **Weekly during active growth:** **20–30 mm — that is 20–30 L/m²** (1 mm over 1 m² = 1 litre). *(The original draft said "200–300 L/m²" — a ×10 unit error.)*
- **Critical periods:** establishment (planting to ~6 weeks) — keep consistently moist; bulbing — slightly drier, never waterlogged.
- **Before harvest:** stop irrigation 10–14 days (2–3 weeks) ahead to mature skins and prevent rot.
- **Method:** drip preferred; overhead watering wets foliage and invites disease.

Too much water: soft bulbs, fungal growth, splitting. Too little: stunting, small bulbs, premature bolting, tip yellowing.

## 6. Pest and disease management

| Threat | Damage | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Onion fly/maggot | Larvae tunnel bulbs; yellowing, stunting | Sticky traps, row covers, rotation |
| Thrips | Silver leaf streaks; virus vector | Blue sticky traps, neem, predatory mites |
| Aphids | Sap-sucking; virus vector | Insecticidal soap, beneficial insects |
| Downy mildew | Yellow patches, downy growth beneath leaves; cool wet weather | Copper (Bordeaux) cover, airflow, avoid overhead watering |
| Purple blotch | Purple-brown leaf spots; warm humid weather | Copper fungicides, 3+ year rotation, debris removal |
| Botrytis neck rot | Storage rot at the neck | Cure properly (2–3 weeks), store cool and dry, cull diseased bulbs |
| White rot | Basal rot with black sclerotia | No effective chemical control — 5+ year rotation, sanitation |

Rotation: 3+ years before alliums return to the same ground. Good followers: leafy greens, legumes, corn, solanaceous crops. Avoid following other alliums.

## 7. Harvest and post-harvest

- **Indicators:** necks soften and tops fall over; leaves yellow from the base; skins papery.
- **Timing:** May–Jun for autumn-planted crops in Kyushu.
- **Cure:** 2–3 weeks in a warm, dry, ventilated place (25–30 °C, 60–70% humidity).
- **Storage:** 3–6 months at 0–5 °C, 60–70% humidity; keep away from apples/pears (ethylene promotes sprouting); inspect and cull regularly.
- **Yield:** ~3–6 kg/m² for mature bulbs.
