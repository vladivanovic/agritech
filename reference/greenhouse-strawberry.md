# Reference — Greenhouse Guide: Strawberries

*Secondary/reference material — greenhouse production is not part of the core orchard plan. Tidied October 2026; one price claim corrected per FACTCHECK.md #63 (see §5).*

## 1. Climate suitability

- **Rating:** ★★★★☆ (High — the Japanese greenhouse strawberry is a forcing-culture staple)
- **Optimal temperature:** 16–24 °C day, 8–13 °C night
- **Growing season:** Nov–Jun (Japanese forcing culture; harvest peak Nov–May)
- **Day length:** Short days induce flowering

## 2. Varieties suitable for Munakata greenhouses

## 3. Greenhouse design for strawberries

### 3.1 Specifications

| Specification | Requirement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 2.0–2.5 m | Working space over elevated beds |
| Width | 6–8 m | 2–3 rows, aisles |
| Length | 20–30 m | Flexible |
| Ventilation | 30–40 air exchanges/hour | Humidity control is critical |
| Temperature control | 8–24 °C range | Heating in winter, ventilation in spring |
| Humidity | 60–70% optimal | Above 80% invites grey mould |

### 3.3 Irrigation and fertilisation

- **Drip irrigation:** 1 emitter per plant, 1–2 L/hour.
- **Fertigation:** balanced N-P-K from transplanting to flowering; potassium-leaning from fruit set; consistent calcium through enlargement.
- **EC:** 1.2–1.8 dS/m · **pH:** 5.8–6.2 (monitor weekly).

## 4. Pest and disease management

- **Major diseases:** Powdery mildew; grey mould (botrytis); root rot (Phytophthora); anthracnose.
- **Major pests:** Spider mites, aphids, whitefly, slugs/snails.
- **Prevention:** Hold humidity at 60–70% (**critical** — mildew risk above 80%); horizontal airflow; ruthless sanitation (remove infected leaves/fruit at once); yellow/blue sticky traps; beneficial insects (predatory mites, parasitic wasps); certified disease-free runners; 1+ year break between strawberry crops in the same house.
- **Lower-input options:** Neem oil, potassium bicarbonate (powdery mildew), *Bacillus subtilis*.

## 5. Harvest and post-harvest

- **Indicators:** Full colour; easy separation with a gentle lift-and-twist; high Brix (Amaou typically in the mid-teens).
- **Frequency:** Every 2–3 days at peak (Nov–May).
- **Yield:** ~2–3 kg/m² per season (elevated gutter systems).
- **Storage:** 1–2 weeks at 4–8 °C, 90–95% humidity (not below 4 °C); do not wash until sale/eating; highly perishable — sell within 2–3 days of harvest.
- **Pick-your-own note:** Picking farms are popular around Munakata. They charge **per person/session or per container**, not by weight in the way the original draft claimed — its "¥1,000–2,000 per 100 g container" figure implied ¥10,000–20,000/kg, which is not credible against Amaou wholesale of ~¥1,200–2,200/kg, and has been **removed**. Verify current local PYO pricing directly before using any figure.

## 7. Annual cycle (forcing culture)

| Season | Task |
|---|---|
| Sep–Oct | Plant new seedlings; prepare the house |
| Nov–Dec | Begin heating; flowering and first fruit |
| Jan–Feb | Peak harvest; maximum heating; pollination management |
| Mar–Apr | Gradual temperature reduction; continued harvest |
| May–Jun | Final harvest; remove plants |
| Jul–Aug | Soil solarisation, soil amendment, fallow (disease break) |
