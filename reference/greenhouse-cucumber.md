# Reference — Greenhouse Guide: Cucumbers

*Secondary/reference material — greenhouse vegetables are not part of the core orchard plan. Lightly tidied October 2026; content not individually flagged in the fact-check.*

## 1. Climate suitability

- **Rating:** ★★★★☆ (High — greenhouse staple crop)
- **Optimal temperature:** 25–30 °C day, 18–20 °C night
- **Growing season:** Mar–Nov (long season, frost-sensitive)
- **Day length:** 12–16 hours; uninterrupted night is important for flowering

## 2. Varieties suitable for greenhouse cultivation

| Type | Variety | Characteristics | Harvest period | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slicing | Summer Glory types | 20–25 cm, dark green, crisp | May–Oct | Common greenhouse type |
| | Minki types | 25–30 cm, smooth skin, sweet | Jun–Nov | Disease resistant |
| Mini | Parisian pickling types | 8–10 cm, bumpy skin | May–Oct | Pickling/snacking |
| | Mini Minki types | 5–8 cm, sweet, thin skin | Jun–Oct | High demand |

For indoor conditions, choose parthenocarpic (female-only) varieties where available.

## 3. Greenhouse design for cucumbers

### 3.1 Specifications

| Specification | Requirement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 2.5–3.0 m | Vine training space |
| Width | 6–8 m | 2–3 rows, aisles |
| Length | 30–50 m | Flexible, depends on scale |
| Ventilation | 40–50 air exchanges/hour | Critical (heat + airflow) |
| Temperature control | 15–35 °C range | Heat-loving, but monitor extremes |

Training: run 1–2 main vines up strings to the overhead rail; remove lateral shoots after the first female flower (variety-dependent); pinch the tip at the target height; remove 1–2 lower leaves per week as the plant grows to keep airflow at the base.

### 3.3 Irrigation and fertilisation

- **Drip irrigation:** 1 emitter per plant (or every 50 cm along the row).
- **Fertigation:** balanced N-P-K to flowering; potassium-leaning from fruit set; consistent feeding through enlargement.
- **EC:** 1.8–2.5 dS/m · **pH:** 5.8–6.2
- **Calcium:** Apply via irrigation to prevent misshapen fruit.

## 4. Pest and disease management

- **Major diseases:** Powdery mildew, downy mildew, angular leaf spot, root rot, cucumber mosaic virus.
- **Major pests:** Aphids, spider mites, whitefly, thrips.
- **Prevention:** 60–70% humidity management; excellent ventilation (horizontal airflow); yellow/blue sticky traps; resistant varieties; remove symptomatic plants immediately; row covers early season (remove at flowering unless parthenocarpic).
- **Lower-input options:** Neem oil, potassium bicarbonate, *Bacillus subtilis*, predatory mites.

## 5. Harvest and post-harvest

- **Indicators:** Size (20–25 cm slicing; 8–10 cm mini), uniform dark-green colour, firm but not hard.
- **Frequency:** Every 2–3 days at peak — frequent picking encourages further production. Harvest early morning for best quality.
- **Yield:** ~3–5 kg/m² per season.
- **Storage:** 1–2 weeks at 10–12 °C, 90–95% humidity. Do **not** store below 10 °C (chilling injury).
