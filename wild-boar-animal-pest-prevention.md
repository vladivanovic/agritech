# Wild Boar and Other Animal Pest Prevention for Munakata, Fukuoka

## 1. Wild Boar (Inoshishi - 豚) - Primary Threat

### 1.1 Damage Assessment
- **Annual damage in Fukuoka**: ¥50M+ across prefecture; Munakata among affected areas
- **Active season**: Year-round, but increased damage Mar-May (spring foraging) and Sep-Nov (autumn fattening)
- **Preferred crops**: Chestnuts, persimmons, corn, vegetables (leafy greens, root vegetables), fallen fruit
- **Damage patterns**: 
  - Nighttime foraging (dawn/dusk most active)
  - Damage edge areas, field borders, overgrown margins
  - Can destroy 10-20% of small orchard in one night

### 1.5 Wild Boar Prevention Priority Order
1. **Fencing** (1.5m minimum, electric if budget allows) - MOST EFFECTIVE
2. **Electric fence** with 3-4 strands as supplementary to mesh
3. **Motion-sensor lights** along perimeter
4. **Noise deterrents** (rotate regularly to prevent habituation)
5. **Trapping/hunting** (last resort, requires permits)

## 2. Other Animal Pests

### 2.1 Japanese Macaque (Saru - サル)
- **Damage**: Fruit trees, field crops; more common in mountainous Munakata areas
- ** Prevention**: Same fencing as boar (1.5m+); add roof/netting for troop access
- **Cost**: Additional ¥200,000-500,000 for roofed enclosure

### 2.2 Deer (Shika - シカ)
- **Damage**: Bark rubbing on young trees, leaf/shoot browsing
- **Prevention**: 
  - Tree guards (1m vinyl wrap around trunk): ¥500-1,000 per tree
  - Fencing: 1.8m minimum (deer jump higher than boar)
  - Repellent sprays: same as boar

### 2.3 Birds (Crows, Bulbul, etc.)
- **Damage**: Peck fruit, damage seedlings, eat seeds
- **Prevention**:
  - **Bird netting**: 10m×10m mesh, ¥15,000-30,000 per 100m²
  - **Scare tape**: Reflective ribbon, ¥3,000-5,000 per 50m
  - **Scare devices**: Same as boar (noise, light, predator calls)

### 2.4 Insect Pests (Already covered in fruit guides)
- **Citrus leafminer, aphids, mites** (mikan)
- **Peach twig borer, brown rot** (peach)
- **Pear psylla, fire blight** (pear)

**Budget for pest control (annual)**: ¥50,000-200,000 depending on scale and infestation level.
